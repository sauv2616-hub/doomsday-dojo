# Doomsday Dojo

A practice game for John Conway's **Doomsday method**: name the weekday of any date in your head.

- **Train** – a 10-step skill ladder (weekday numbers → mod 7 → leap years → month doomsdays → century anchors → year offset → year doomsday → full dates), with 20-question rounds, 60-second blitz, or endless mode. Live timer, instant feedback with the worked reasoning, and adaptive repetition of the items you miss.
- **Guided walkthrough** – solve a date step by step, each step checked as you enter it.
- **Daily five** – five fixed dates per day; beat your best time.
- **Solve & check** – enter any date and your answer; see every step (12s method and Odd+11), a diagnosis of your mistake, and the month calendar.
- **Learn** – lessons, cheat sheet, and a year-by-doomsday table.

Add to your home screen from Safari (Share → Add to Home Screen). Works offline; progress is stored on your device.
